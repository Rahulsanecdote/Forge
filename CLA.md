## Why a CLA at all?

The honest answer: without it, the project could never offer a commercial licence,
because doing so would require tracking down and getting permission from every past
contributor. Projects that skip this step and later need it have to rewrite or delete
other people's work. Asking up front is the less unpleasant option.

If you would rather not sign, you can still open issues, file bug reports, review
pull requests, and discuss design — none of that needs a CLA.
